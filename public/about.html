About Easy Scanlate

The ultimate solution for enhancing your productivity and streamlining your scanlation workflow.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide an intuitive, powerful, and elegant tool that helps you focus on what truly matters: quality translations. We believe that automation should empower creators, not replace them.

Our Story

Founded in 2025 by a team of passionate developers and scanlation enthusiasts, Easy Scanlate was born out of a simple need: a better way to work. We were tired of juggling multiple applications for OCR, translation, and cleaning. We knew there had to be a more efficient and enjoyable way to manage our daily tasks. So, we decided to build it ourselves.

Our Philosophy

We believe that software should be a joy to use. It should be fast, reliable, and beautiful. Every feature we build, every design decision we make, is guided by our commitment to user experience. We listen to our users, and we are constantly working to improve our application based on your feedback.
